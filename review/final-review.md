# Final review

Final review is the last gate before `DONE`. Confirm that Phase 2 review and `FINAL-QUALITY-REPORT.md` passed, the active structure lock is traceable to the Phase 1 artifact versions it protects, the expected delivery is rendered and available, and `FINAL-REVIEW.md` records the result.

- Implementation or visual delivery issue: return to `PHASE_2`.
- Structural or business requirement issue: return to `PHASE_1`.
- Missing evidence or an unresolved conflict: enter `BLOCKED`.
- Pass: set `FINAL-REVIEW.md` status to `passed` and transition to `DONE`.
