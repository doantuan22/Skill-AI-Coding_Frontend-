# Forms

Use for data collection, registration, configuration, or input-heavy steps. Decide field order, grouping, required versus optional data, whether multiple steps are justified, validation timing, error placement, progress indication, and completion feedback. Keep labels as explicit field identity; do not use placeholders as the only label.

Model the form’s default, validation/error, disabled, saving, success, cancellation, and permission states when relevant. Do not mark data required without a confirmed business reason.
