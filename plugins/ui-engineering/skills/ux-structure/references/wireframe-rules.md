# Wireframe rules

Wireframes communicate structural hierarchy, not visual treatment. Name page sections and position navigation, context, primary action, secondary actions, main content, feedback, and states. Group related fields or data, keep actions near their decision context, and use progressive disclosure for secondary detail.

Describe responsive transformation intent only at a semantic level: what must persist, what may collapse, and what changes ordering. Do not specify CSS, breakpoint values, fonts, color, decoration, or pixel-perfect geometry.
