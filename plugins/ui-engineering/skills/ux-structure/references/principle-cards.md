# Principle cards

| Principle | When it applies | Problem | Recommended response | Exception |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recognition over recall | A decision needs prior-screen knowledge | User must remember context | Surface necessary context where action occurs | Do not duplicate sensitive or irrelevant data. |
| Progressive disclosure | Detail serves only a subset or later decision | Everything competes at once | Show essential context first; reveal detail on demand | Keep required data/action visible. |
| Clear responsibility | A page has unrelated goals | User cannot tell what to do | Split, reorganize, or make one goal primary | Related sub-actions may remain together. |
| Reversible action | User may make a non-destructive mistake | Recovery is unclear | Provide confirmation, undo, or a return path | Do not weaken necessary irreversible safeguards. |
| Consistent language | One concept appears across flows | Terms imply false differences | Use the approved term from the registry | Preserve legally distinct concepts. |
| State-aware flow | Data/action can be unavailable | Only happy path is designed | Specify empty, error, loading, and recovery paths | Omit states impossible under confirmed constraints. |
