# Avoid generic AI aesthetics (compatibility entry)

The Visual Language layer is the Phase 2 source of truth for anti-generic review. Load [global review](../visual-language/anti-slop/global.md) and [AI tell density](../visual-language/ai-tell-density.md), then only the targeted color, surface, icon, motion or composition reference. A visual tell is a signal, not a blanket ban: retain a deliberate, accessible and task-fitting choice; remove unexplained clusters and document exceptions in `VISUAL-GRAMMAR.md`.
