## 1. SCSS Organization & Modern Modules
- **Modern Imports**: Prefer `@use` and `@forward` over legacy `@import`.
- **Variables & Mixins**: Place design variables (`$color-primary`, `$spacing-unit`) in a centralized `_variables.scss` or `_tokens.scss`.
- **Nesting Discipline**: Do not nest deeper than 3 levels to prevent overly specific CSS selectors.
