# Design direction rules

Direction must be task-appropriate and explicitly tied to the Design Brief and lock. Respect explicit visual requests; when they conflict with readability, accessibility, or locked semantics, preserve the intent through a safer realization.

Avoid generic defaults—unmotivated purple/blue gradients, ubiquitous glass, arbitrary shadows, floating blobs, huge hero text without purpose, card-everything layouts, pill-everything controls, and motion without feedback value. Intentional style is allowed when its purpose and constraints are recorded.
